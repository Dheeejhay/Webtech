Baguio City's cold mountain air does more than just make you reach for a jacket—it builds an incredible appetite. Known as the summer capital of the Philippines, Baguio has evolved into a powerhouse culinary destination. The city's food scene seamlessly weaves fresh, farm-to-table Benguet produce, centuries-old indigenous Cordilleran cooking, and creative international concepts introduced by a diverse community of expats and young chefs. Whether you are hunting for cozy mountainside cafes or hearty family-style platters, here is an insider look at the restaurants you cannot miss.
